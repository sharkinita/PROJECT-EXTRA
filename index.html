About Project EXTRA

Valugan Integrated School System Architecture

Project EXTRA (Electronic e-Xpedited Tracking, Review & Approval System) is designed to streamline school reporting, eliminate paper waste, and provide real-time sequential approval tracking for Valugan Integrated School.

System Proponents:

• Sharlynne Mae P. Veracruz-Francisco

• Monica V. Castillo
